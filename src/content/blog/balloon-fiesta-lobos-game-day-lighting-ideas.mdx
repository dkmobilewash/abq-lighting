## Why Permanent Lighting Is Perfect for These Occasions

With traditional strands, nobody hangs lights for a single week in October or a Saturday football game. It is not worth the ladder. With permanent lighting, the lights are already on your house, so switching your roofline to a new palette takes a few taps in the app.

That flexibility is what makes the in-between occasions so much fun. Your home can glow warm white on an ordinary Tuesday, burst into balloon colors for Fiesta week, switch to cherry and silver before kickoff, and go back to warm white when it is all over.

## Balloon Fiesta Lighting Ideas

Balloon Fiesta happens in early October, and the city wakes up before dawn to watch mass ascensions over the north side of town. Homes in the Northeast Heights, Sandia Heights and along the West Side often get a view of balloons drifting across the sky in the early morning. Your lighting can carry that spirit into the evening.

### Rainbow Envelope Chase

Balloons come in every color imaginable, so lean into it. Set a multi-color pattern of red, orange, yellow, green, blue and purple, and use a slow chase effect so the colors drift along your roofline like balloons moving across the sky. Keep the speed gentle so it feels festive rather than frantic.

### Dawn Patrol Glow

The earliest balloons launch in the dark and glow from their burners against the night sky. Recreate that feeling with a warm amber and soft orange palette on a slow pulse effect. It is subtle, beautiful, and works well if you want something more refined than a full rainbow.

### Sunrise Over the Sandias

Fiesta mornings often start with pink and gold light spreading over the mountains. A palette of watermelon pink, soft coral and golden yellow captures that moment. Set it on a gentle fade for an elegant look that fits any home style, from Pueblo Revival to a newer two-story stucco build.

### Balloon Glow Evenings

Evening glow events at Balloon Fiesta Park are a highlight of the week. At home, try alternating blocks of bright, saturated colors with a soft twinkle effect. It gives your roofline the feeling of a field full of glowing envelopes.

## Lobos Game Day Lighting Ideas

Whether it is football in the fall or basketball at The Pit in the winter, Lobos fans across the metro love to show their colors. Cherry and silver look sharp on just about any roofline.

### Classic Cherry and Silver

The simplest option is often the best. Alternate cherry red with a cool silver-white in even blocks along your roofline. It reads clearly from the street and instantly tells the neighbors where your loyalties are.

### Kickoff Chase

For the hours before a game, set cherry and silver on a moving chase effect. It adds energy to tailgate gatherings in your driveway or backyard and makes your house a natural meeting spot for friends heading out together.

### Victory Pulse

If the game goes your way, switch to a bright cherry pulse for the rest of the evening. Save it as a favorite scene in the app so you can activate it the moment the final whistle blows.

### Steady Cherry for Game Week

Some fans prefer a solid color all week leading up to a big rivalry game. A steady cherry red on the front roofline, with warm white on the patio or portal for everyday use, gives you team spirit without giving up practical lighting where you need it.

## Scheduling Tips So You Never Forget

The best part of permanent lighting is that you can set it once and let it run.

**Schedule Fiesta week in advance.** Once the Fiesta dates are announced, set your balloon scene to start at sunset each evening for the whole week, then automatically return to warm white afterward.

**Set game day scenes by date.** At the start of the season, add each home game to your schedule with your favorite cherry and silver scene. The lights will switch on their own before kickoff.

**Use sunset timing.** The app can follow sunset times, so your lights turn on at the right moment even as October evenings get shorter.

**Keep everyday lighting in other zones.** If your system has multiple zones, keep warm white on the entry, portal or patio for safety and comfort while the main roofline shows team or Fiesta colors.

## A Few Design Tips for Great Results

**Fewer colors can look better from the street.** Two or three colors in clear blocks often read more cleanly than a long rainbow, especially on a single-story ranch home.

**Match the effect to the mood.** Slow fades feel elegant, while fast chases feel energetic. Use the faster effects for short windows like pregame and the calmer ones for all-week scenes.

**Think about your neighbors.** Moderate brightness and calmer effects late in the evening keep things friendly, especially in close-knit neighborhoods or HOA communities.
